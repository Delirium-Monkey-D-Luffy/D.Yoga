## **INTRO – CASTLE, 90s – NIGHT**

**LOCATION:** A luxurious 60 m² living room in a castle. 90s decor: thick carpet, heavy wooden furniture, an old square CRT TV on a low stand. Movie posters (*Terminator 2, Robocop*) are pinned to the walls.

- **VINCE (17)**, tall, dressed in jeans and a rock band t-shirt.
- **BOB (12)**, small, energetic, wearing a basketball t-shirt.
- **MONKEY (4/5)**, wearing a **blue tank top with a large white "42"** on the chest.

**ACTION:**
The three brothers are kneeling in front of the TV. Vince is installing a **pirate decoder** on the antenna, connected by a mess of wires.

**SCENE:**Vince makes the final connections. Bob holds a flashlight. Monkey, sitting cross-legged, watches with intense excitement.

> **BOB**
> (laughing)
> You're a little monkey, huh! But you're a wimp who doesn't understand anything about life, you little fag!

Monkey ignores the remark, hypnotized by the TV.

> **MONKEY**
> And lots of movies with swords and spears!! There'll be robots, I love robots! Vince, how did you get Canal+ for free???

Vince, proud, turns to his little brother.

> **VINCE**
> It's simple, because I'm a... PIRATE!!! I steal from the rich to enjoy their privileges, ahahah!

He gets closer, taps Monkey's head.

> **VINCE** (CONT)
> So, my little MONKEY! Isn't that chan-MEEE?

> **MONKEY**
> (repeating dumbly)
> Chan-mee?? What does that mean?

> **VINCE**
> It's a term that I, the LORD of the pirates of the seas and the Carpathians, luminous and messianic genius, first grand supreme and enlightened citizen of the Evangelical Islamic Republic of the ancestral arts of the Phoenix...

> **VINCE** (CONT)
> ...invented!
> (pointing at himself, proud)

Monkey repeats it, louder and louder, laughing.

> **MONKEY**
> It's chan MEEE, ahahah, it's CHANMEE, ahaha, it's too CHAN ME, ahhah! It's like that other word you taught me: « COOOOOL », ahahhaah!

He repeats both words on a loop. Vince and Bob settle on the couch, start *Robocop*. Monkey lies down in front of the TV, head in his hands, a blissful smile on his lips.

**CLOSE UP on Monkey's face.**His eyes shine with dreams, hope, magic.

> **MONKEY**
> (soft, determined voice)
> It's so cool and CHAN MEE! ... When I grow up, I WANT TO BE A...

An almost demonic smile crosses his face.

> **MONKEY** (CONT)
> PIRATE!! And I'll be the one who becomes the PIRATE LORD! Ahah, because it's CHAN MEEE and so cooool! Ahahahahaha!
