**DTC/DAO Context:** You try to mask the system's flaws (the Sun) with brilliant communication, but the truth is veiled. The Reversed Sun is the symbol of **centralized propaganda** that can no longer hide the reality of the rising chaos.

**The Outcome (Future): The Upright Hermit.**
**Myth:** After the fall, redemption. Like **Diogenes** searching for the honest man, you will find wisdom in withdrawal. The Hermit is your only way out: a constructive retreat, far from political turmoil, where lasting influence is gained in the shadows.
