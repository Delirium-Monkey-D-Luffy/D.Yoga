# Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

---

## 🌸 Phase 1: Basic Setup (1–2 Weeks)

**Objective:** Set up the core operational structure of D.LNSD.

**Tools:**

- Dedicated Discord / Telegram for initial Pods
- 3–5 volunteer Sadhakas
- Coordination by D.Nami

**MVP Smart Contracts:**

- Fork of the **D.Flow** contract (from D.AI-RT)
- Simplified **Lila Enforcer** contract on testnet (Ethereum / Polygon)

**Test Content:**

- Produce 5 prototype memes
  - Example: “DTC Explained with Cats & Curves”
- Sourced from D.AI-RT Meme Pod

---

## 🚀 Phase 2: First Drop (Weeks 3–4)

**Objective:** Launch first viral campaigns and validate the reward model.

**Launch:**

- Publish 3 ads on X / TikTok
- Example: “Discover the White Mirror… no mirror, just dharmic fun!”

**Measurement:**

- Track via **DharmaPulse** (minimum 1,000 engagements per campaign)
- Reward: 10 $D.Flow per validated hit

**Collaboration:**

- Integrate 2 assets from D.AI-RT:
  1. 1 ethical deepfake
  2. 1 dharmically approved joke

---

## 🌱 Phase 3: Scale & Feedback (Month 2)

**Objective:** Structure the Pods and test system scalability.

**Active Pods:**

- Onboard 10 creators via DAO vote
- Roles: Meme-Magic, Boob-Dharma, Viral-Tech

**Success Metrics:**

- +20% DTC adoption (wallet connects)
- Virality > 50,000 views → unlock **“Boob-Dharma Level 1”**

**Risk Management:**

- If content is too “spicy” → auto-transfer to **Sith Vault** for iteration
