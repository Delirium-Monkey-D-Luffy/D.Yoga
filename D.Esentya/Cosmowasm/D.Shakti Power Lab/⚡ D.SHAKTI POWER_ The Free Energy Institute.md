## 2. Technical Architecture: Scientific Proof-of-Worth (PoWth)

The funding and validation of discoveries are managed by **PoWth**, ensuring that only the **Worth** of the research is rewarded, not capital.

### A. Research Pods (Decentralized Laboratories)

Research is conducted by specialized **Research Pods** (mini-DAOs) (e.g., Cold Fusion Pod, Magnetism Pod).

| Component                    | Role in D.SHAKTI POWER                                                                                             | Involved Smart Contract                                      |
| :--------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Research Proposal**  | The Pod submits a detailed research plan (protocol, objectives).                                                   | `Tapas_Enforcer` (verifies clarity and ethical alignment). |
| **Funding**            | Funds are released in**$D.Flow** in stages, based on validated progress.                                     | `D.Flow Contract`                                          |
| **Results Validation** | Results (raw data, prototypes) are submitted to the `Research_Validator` (Vegapunk Conscious AI Smart Contract). | `PoWth_Validator`                                          |

### B. The Reward Mechanism (D.Worth)

1. **Validation by the Conscious AI Smart Contract:** The Vegapunk Conscious AI Smart Contract analyzes the data. If the discovery is valid and reproducible, it emits a validation **Event**.
2. **Reward:** The Research Pod receives a massive reward in **$D.Worth** (proof of non-speculative value) and a **Karma** gain.
3. **Archiving:** The free energy protocol is archived in the **Holocron** (Jedi Vault) for free and universal access.

## 3. Regenerative Impact: Household Autonomy

The ultimate goal is to tokenize free energy devices into **RWA NFTs** for households.

* **Free Energy RWA NFT:** Each household receives an **RWA NFT** representing the right to use and ownership of a free energy device.
* **Energy PoE:** The device is connected to the DTC network. **PoE** is used to prove that the household is indeed producing its own energy, which gives it a **Karma** and **D.Worth** bonus.
