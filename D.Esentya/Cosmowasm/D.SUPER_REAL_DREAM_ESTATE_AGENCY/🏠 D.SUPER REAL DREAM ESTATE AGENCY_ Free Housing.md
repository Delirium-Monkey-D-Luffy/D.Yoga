## 3. The Peaceful Transition of Real Estate Assets

The Agency manages two asset flows: the construction of new Pods and the redistribution of existing assets.

### A. Construction of New Pods

DTC members build their own Housing Pods (according to Galley La Company plans) using resources financed by Karma Loans from the Pirate Bank and D.SHAKTI energy.

### B. Redistribution of Existing Assets

This is D. Coco's role. The Agency offers old-world owners an integration plan:
1.  **Asset Conversion:** The real estate asset is valued and converted into $D.Worth (according to a fair, non-speculative rate).
2.  **Community Usage:** The asset is integrated into the DTC network for community use (housing, Production Pod, D.Delirium School, etc.).
3.  **Usage Guarantee:** The former owner receives a lifetime usage guarantee (not ownership) within the DTC network, securing their housing and Karma.
