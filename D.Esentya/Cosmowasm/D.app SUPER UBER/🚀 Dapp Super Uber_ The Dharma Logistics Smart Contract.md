## 2. Technological Architecture: The Karma Network

The Dapp Super Uber uses the **D.Esentya Protocol** to transform travel time into karmic value.

### A. The Karma Points Mechanism

*   **Time Validation:** Every driver (RATP, Velib, VTC, etc.) and every passenger who uses a shared or ecological mode of transport validates their travel time via the Dapp.
*   **Karmic Compensation:** The validated time is converted into **Karma Points** on the user's **Truth ID**. These points are exchangeable for DTC services (housing, food, energy).
*   **Goal:** To make transport **free** in the long term, with value generated by network optimization and accumulated Karma.

### B. The Echopods: Fleet Decentralization

*   **Concept:** Echopods are charging and vehicle-sharing stations (electric cars, bikes, scooters) strategically installed based on the living locations of drivers (e.g., near Charles de Gaulle Airport).
*   **Role:** To allow drivers to leave their personal vehicle at a central point for use by other DTC members, thus optimizing fleet utilization and serving villages and small towns.
