## 1. Philosophy: Work as Sadhana (Svadhyaya & Ishvara Pranidhana)

Work is not an economic constraint, but an expression of one's Dharma (one's role in the universe). The Factory's mission is to align the talent of each individual with the community's need.

> **Mantra:** *Your job is not what you do to live, it's what you do to be. Mahamahmamaha!*
