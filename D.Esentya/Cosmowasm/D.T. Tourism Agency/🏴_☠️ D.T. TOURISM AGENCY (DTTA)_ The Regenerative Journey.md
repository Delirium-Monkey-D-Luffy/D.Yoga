## 2. Technical Architecture: Decentralized GPS (PoE)

The DTTA relies on an inviolable geolocation mechanism to guarantee the authenticity of interactions.

### A. Proof-of-Esentya (PoE) for Tourism

The **PoE** is the Smart Contract that validates the physical presence of the traveler and the local guide.

1.  **The Traveler (Explorer):** Uses their mobile device to emit a presence signal (BLE, GPS, Wi-Fi Triangulation) at a point of interest.
2.  **The Local Guide (Guardian):** Uses their device to co-sign the presence, proving that they provided the service.
3.  **Validation:** The `PoE_Beacon_Manager` on CosmWasm validates the transaction.

### B. The Role of Local Pods

Each destination (Bali, Nepal, Brazil) is managed by a **Local Pod** (mini-DAO) that:

*   **Creates Quests:** Tokenizes local services (temple guide, cooking class) into **RWA NFT Quests**.
*   **Manages Guides:** Validates the **Guardian of the Place** status (Eternal Sadhaka) of local guides.
